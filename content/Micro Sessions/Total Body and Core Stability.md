## Warm Up
- [[Cat - Cow]] for 1 minute
- *Rest 30 seconds*
- [[Bird - Dog]] 1 set of 10 reps
- *Rest 30 seconds*

## Circuit
- [[Glute Bridge]] 1 set of 12 reps
- *Rest 15 seconds*
- [[Alternating Bent-over Row with Dumbbells]] 1 set of 12 reps each side
- *Rest 15 seconds*
- [[Seated Overhead Press with Dumbbell]] 1 set of 12 reps each side
- *Rest 15 seconds*
- [[Deadbugs]] 1 set of 12 reps
- *Rest 60 seconds and repeat circuit*
