## Warm Up
- [[Cat - Cow]] for 1 minute
- [[Bird - Dog]] 1 set of 12 reps
- *Rest 30 seconds*

## Circuit
- [[Deadbugs]] 1 set of 12 reps
- *Rest 15 seconds*
- [[Wall Sit]] for 45 seconds
- *Rest 15 seconds*
- [[Bicep Curls with Dumbbells]] 1 set of 12 reps
- *Rest 1 minute and repeat circuit* 2 times

## Finisher
- [[Glute Bridge]] 1 set of 12 reps
